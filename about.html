Manufacturing Premium Adhesive Tapes Since 2010

Kosha Tapes is the trusted adhesive tape brand of Tiger Tapes and Packaging Company, founded in 2010 by Mr. Pushkar Raj. Based in Chennai, we manufacture and supply a full range of adhesive tapes — including BOPP packaging tape, masking tape, foam tape, insulation tape and colour tapes — to businesses across Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh.

What began as a small manufacturing unit has grown into a dependable supply partner for distributors, wholesalers, e-commerce sellers and industrial units, built on a simple promise: consistent adhesion, reliable delivery, and honest service.
