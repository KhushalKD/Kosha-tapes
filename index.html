Why Choose Us

Reliable Packaging Solutions You Can Trust

Since 2010, Tiger Tapes and Packaging Company has been manufacturing and supplying premium-quality adhesive tapes under the Kosha Tapes brand. With a strong focus on quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we deliver durable tape solutions that meet the packaging requirements of businesses across a wide range of industries.

From Packing Tapes and BOPP Jumbo Rolls to Masking Tapes, Foam Tapes, Insulation Tapes, and Colour Tapes, every product is manufactured using high-quality materials and strict quality standards to ensure excellent adhesion, durability, and consistent performance. Our commitment to reliable manufacturing, timely delivery, and responsive customer support has made us a trusted partner for distributors, wholesalers, and businesses seeking dependable packaging solutions.
